## 1. Executive Mission & Scope Architecture

The objective of Part B is to conduct exhaustive, evidence-led research into how elite transaction advisory firms (Big 4, Alvarez & Marsal, Kroll, RSM, BDO, Houlihan Lokey) execute financial due diligence. We must understand the actual operational procedures, calculations, and judgment calls before writing simulator specifications.

The research is organized across **Three Core Dimensions**:
1. **Dimension A — Professional Methodology:** Common engagement procedures across all deals (data intake, GL extraction, proof of cash, EBITDA bridges, working capital pegs, and report generation).
2. **Dimension B — Client-Specific Financial Reality:** How accounting systems and transaction risks diverge across our three designated client industries:
   * **Client Type A:** *Distribution & Wholesale* (inventory turns, supplier terms, rebate accruals, credit notes, sales cut-off).
   * **Client Type B:** *Manufacturing* (raw materials, WIP, finished goods, cost of goods manufactured, labor/overhead absorption, scrap).
   * **Client Type C:** *SaaS & Software* (ARR/MRR waterfalls, gross/net retention, deferred revenue haircuts, CAC, ASC 606 recognition).
3. **Dimension C — Engagement Execution:** Operational workflows, team hierarchy (Partner to Associate), virtual data room management, Q&A protocols, review cycles, and deliverable production.

## 3. The Seven Strict Quality Gates (G1 to G7)

No workstream is certified complete unless it satisfies all seven quality gates:
* **G1 (Conceptual Understanding):** Professional purpose, legal perimeter, and operational rationale explained without generic fluff.
* **G2 (Source Validation):** Claims anchored in authoritative professional standards (ICAEW, AICPA, FASB, Big 4 manuals).
* **G3 (Real-World Evidence):** Authenticated real-world templates, schemas, and practitioner artifacts inspected.
* **G4 (Technical Understanding):** Mathematical formulas, format specifications, and control checks documented.
* **G5 (Operational Understanding):** Separation of duties, workflow steps, and timeline gates established.
* **G6 (Investigative Understanding):** Exception modes, deficiency registers, management pushback, and escalation rules mapped.
* **G7 (Simulation Readiness):** Concrete relational database schemas, state machines, and generation rules defined.
