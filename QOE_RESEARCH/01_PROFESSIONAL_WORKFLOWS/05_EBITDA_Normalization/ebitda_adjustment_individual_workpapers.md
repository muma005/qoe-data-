### ADJ-001: Transaction-Related Advisory & Legal Retainers
* **Category:** Transaction-Related Expenses (`ADJ-CAT-01`)
* **Financial Statement Line:** SG&A — Legal & Professional Fees (`GL 6-2100`)
* **Vendor / Payee:** Kirkland & Apex Advisory Partners LLC
* **Document Reference:** `INV-MNA-8842` / `ENG-KIRK-2023`
* **Period:** FY2023 (Incurred August – November 2023)

#### 1. Background & Management Claim
Management proposed a $145,000 add-back for advisory fees paid to M&A legal counsel and sell-side transaction brokers in connection with preparing Apex Global Industrial for the current sale process. Management asserted that these costs are strictly deal-dependent, will not recur under post-closing operations, and have no operational utility to ongoing business functions.

#### 2. Accounting Trace & General Ledger Verification
The general ledger was queried for account `6-2100`. Diligence identified three specific debit entries:
* `2023-08-15` | JE #4412 | Debit: $50,000.00 | Retainer payment to Kirkland Advisory
* `2023-10-12` | JE #4890 | Debit: $45,000.00 | M&A legal due diligence preparation
* `2023-11-28` | JE #5310 | Debit: $50,000.00 | Confidential Information Memorandum (CIM) assembly
* **Total General Ledger Debits Verified:** **$145,000.00**

#### 3. Source Document Inventory & Audit Trail
* `ENG-KIRK-2023`: Executed engagement letter dated July 10, 2023, defining scope strictly as "Transaction Advisory & Strategic Alternatives Analysis."
* Invoices #KA-1088, #KA-1142, #KA-1201: Detailed itemized attorney hours confirming time spent drafting data room documents, organizing corporate charter files, and responding to buyer queries.
* Canceled check copies & bank debit statements confirming cash settlement from corporate operating account.

Diligence verified that these fees were expensed directly to P&L account `6-2100` and were **not** capitalized on the balance sheet under Deferred Transaction Costs (`GL 1-1850`). Concurrently, diligence verified that these advisory costs will not be paid post-closing as transaction bonuses or debt-like closing costs.

### ADJ-003: Owner / Executive Compensation Replacement Benchmarking

* **Financial Statement Line:** SG&A — Officer Salaries (`GL 6-1100`)
* **Payee:** John Apex (Founder, CEO & 85% Controlling Shareholder)
* **Document Reference:** `W2-2023-EXEC-01` / `MERCER-BENCH-2023` / `EMP-AGR-JAPEX`
* **Period:** FY2023

#### 1. Background & Management Claim
Management claimed a full $450,000 add-back for the founder/CEO's cash salary, arguing that John Apex intends to retire at transaction closing and will draw zero salary post-closing. Management proposed adding back 100% of his historical compensation to Reported EBITDA.

#### 2. Accounting Trace & General Ledger Verification
Examination of `GL 6-1100` and 2023 IRS Form W-2 (Box 1 and Box 5) confirmed John Apex received $450,000.00 in gross executive payroll distributions across 24 bi-weekly pay periods ($18,750 per period).

#### 3. Source Document Inventory & Audit Trail
* 2023 IRS Form W-2, Box 1: $450,000.00.
* Employer payroll tax register: FICA/Medicare tax paid on behalf of CEO ($15,450.00).
* Mercer Executive Compensation Survey (2023 Benchmark for Industrial Wholesale & Distribution, $30M–$60M Revenue Segment, Code 11-1011 Chief Executives).
* Target Company job description and organizational chart confirming John Apex acts as active General Manager directing operations, vendor negotiations, and key accounts.

#### 4. Analytical Findings & Judgment Discussion
**Management's 100% add-back claim is aggressive and unsupportable.** While John Apex may retire, the company cannot operate without a Chief Executive Officer / General Manager. A qualified third-party replacement executive must be hired.
Diligence established the fair market value (FMV) replacement package based on Mercer 50th percentile benchmarks:
* Benchmark Base Salary: $200,000.00
* Target Performance Bonus (20%): $40,000.00
* Employer Payroll Taxes & Benefits Load (4.17%): $10,000.00
* **Total Replacement Executive Cost:** **$250,000.00**

Subtracting the $250,000 replacement cost from the historical $450,000 compensation yields a defensible net add-back of **$200,000.00**. The remaining $250,000 is rejected.

#### 5. Double-Counting & Balance Sheet Interaction Check
Diligence confirmed that CEO auto allowance ($12,000/yr) was tracked in Travel & Entertainment (`GL 6-1500`) and has not been added back, ensuring no overlapping double add-back.

#### 6. Mathematical Determination
$$\begin{array}{llr}
\text{Management Claimed Add-Back:} & & \$450,000.00 \\
\text{Historical Officer Cash Compensation:} & & \$450,000.00 \\
\text{Less: FMV Replacement Executive Cost:} & & -\$250,000.00 \\
\hline
\mathbf{Accepted\ QoE\ Add-Back\ (Net):} & & \mathbf{\$200,000.00} \\

### ADJ-004: Related-Party Facility Rent Normalization

* **Document Reference:** `LEASE-2018-PLANT` / `CUSHMAN-APPRAISAL-2023`
* **Period:** FY2023

#### 1. Background & Management Claim
Management proposed a $120,000 add-back for facility lease rent paid to Apex Realty Holdings LLC for the main distribution warehouse in Gary, Indiana. Management claimed the property will be included in the transaction or renegotiated down to zero post-close.

#### 2. Accounting Trace & General Ledger Verification
Review of `GL 6-2300` confirmed 12 monthly rent payments of $10,000.00 directly to Apex Realty Holdings LLC, totaling $120,000.00 ($12.00/sq ft for 10,000 sq ft of warehouse/office space).

#### 3. Source Document Inventory & Audit Trail
* Commercial Lease Agreement dated January 1, 2018 (`LEASE-2018-PLANT`).
* Independent Commercial Real Estate Appraisal prepared by Cushman & Wakefield (`CUSHMAN-APPRAISAL-2023`), assessing local industrial warehouse lease comps within a 15-mile radius.
* County tax assessor records and utility invoices.

#### 4. Analytical Findings & Judgment Discussion
The company requires the warehouse to conduct distribution operations. The property is **not** being transferred with the operating entity; the buyer must enter into a new market-rate commercial lease with Apex Realty Holdings LLC at closing.
The Cushman & Wakefield appraisal established the current Fair Market Rent (FMV) at **$6.00 per sq ft NNN**, or **$60,000.00 annually**.
Therefore:
* Historical Contractual Rent Expensed: $120,000.00
* Fair Market Value Commercial Rent: $60,000.00
* **Permissible Net Add-Back:** **$60,000.00**
* The remaining $60,000.00 represents necessary ongoing operational occupancy cost and is rejected.

#### 5. Double-Counting & Balance Sheet Interaction Check
Diligence reviewed property taxes ($14,200) and property insurance ($6,800) in `GL 6-2350`. The historical lease was modified Gross; the new lease will be NNN. When adjusted, the ongoing base rent plus NNN expenses align with local commercial standards.

#### 6. Mathematical Determination
$$\begin{array}{llr}
\text{Management Claimed Add-Back:} & & \$120,000.00 \\

### ADJ-005: Extraordinary Facility Storm Roof Repair
* **Category:** Extraordinary Repairs & Casualty Losses (`ADJ-CAT-05`)
* **Financial Statement Line:** Operating Expenses — Repairs & Maintenance (`GL 6-2400`)
* **Payee:** Midwest Industrial Roofing Inc.
* **Document Reference:** `INV-ROOF-9921` / `INS-CLAIM-7741`
* **Period:** FY2023 (Incurred June 2023)

#### 1. Background & Management Claim
Management proposed a $95,000 add-back representing emergency roof replacement and water remediation costs incurred in June 2023 following a severe hailstorm that punctured the warehouse roof and flooded the loading dock. Management classified this as an extraordinary, unpreventable casualty event.

#### 2. Accounting Trace & General Ledger Verification
Inspection of `GL 6-2400` verified invoice #9921 from Midwest Industrial Roofing debited on June 22, 2023, in the amount of $95,000.00.

#### 3. Source Document Inventory & Audit Trail
* Contractor Invoice `INV-ROOF-9921` detailing emergency labor, membrane patching, and structural deck repairs.
* National Weather Service local storm report confirming severe hail and wind in Lake County, IN, on June 14, 2023.
* Commercial Insurance Claim File `INS-CLAIM-7741` submitted to Hartford Commercial Insurance.
* Settlement remittance advice from Hartford confirming insurance reimbursement of **$55,000.00** ($95,000 claim less $15,000 deductible less $25,000 policy depreciation cap).

#### 4. Analytical Findings & Judgment Discussion
Management added back the full gross invoice of $95,000 in their pitch book EBITDA bridge. However, general ledger research revealed that the $55,000 insurance proceeds were received in September 2023 and credited to **Other Income** (`GL 7-1000`).
Allowing a $95,000 add-back in operating expenses while simultaneously retaining the $55,000 insurance reimbursement in earnings inflates total adjusted earnings by $55,000.
The true unrecovered economic casualty cost to the company was:
$$\text{Net Unrecovered Expense} = \$95,000.00 - \$55,000.00 = \$40,000.00$$
Therefore, only the **$40,000.00 net out-of-pocket casualty loss** is accepted as an operating expense add-back. Concurrently, the $55,000 insurance credit in Other Income must be eliminated (see ADJ-006).

#### 5. Double-Counting & Balance Sheet Interaction Check
Diligence confirmed that Midwest Industrial Roofing repair was not capitalized in Fixed Assets.

#### 6. Mathematical Determination
$$\begin{array}{llr}
\text{Management Claimed Add-Back:} & & \$95,000.00 \\
\text{Gross Repair Invoice Expensed:} & & \$95,000.00 \\
\text{Less: Insurance Reimbursement Offset:} & & -\$55,000.00 \\
\hline
\mathbf{Accepted\ QoE\ Add-Back\ (Net):} & & \mathbf{\$40,000.00} \\
\mathbf{Rejected\ Amount:} & & \mathbf{\$55,000.00} \\
\text{Classification:} & & \text{Accepted-Partial}
\end{array}$$

### ADJ-007: Customer Bad Debt & Commercial Credit Loss
* **Category:** Customer Bad Debt Normalization (`ADJ-CAT-08`)
* **Financial Statement Line:** SG&A — Bad Debt Expense (`GL 6-2500`)
* **Debtor / Customer:** Great Plains Logistics Corp
* **Document Reference:** `COURT-BK-2023-11` / `AR-SUB-GP-2023`
* **Period:** FY2023 (Written off November 2023)

#### 1. Background & Management Claim
Management claimed a $110,000 add-back for an accounts receivable write-off resulting from the sudden Chapter 11 bankruptcy filing of customer Great Plains Logistics Corp. Management argued this was a "black swan" event, uncharacteristic of their customer portfolio, and should be excluded from ongoing operational earnings.

#### 2. Accounting Trace & General Ledger Verification
Review of `GL 6-2500` confirmed journal entry #5120 on November 14, 2023:
* Debit: Bad Debt Expense (`6-2500`) $110,000.00
* Credit: Allowance for Doubtful Accounts (`1-1150`) $110,000.00

#### 3. Source Document Inventory & Audit Trail
* U.S. Bankruptcy Court filing notice, Case #23-BK-1104, confirming Great Plains Logistics Chapter 11 filing on October 29, 2023.
* Historical AR aging reports and default customer correspondence.

#### 4. Analytical Findings & Judgment Discussion
**Diligence rejected this add-back in its entirety.**
In commercial wholesale distribution, extending trade credit (Net 30/60 days) is an inherent and unavoidable operational risk of doing business. An analysis of historical bad debt expense across the historical review period revealed:
* FY2021 Bad Debt Expense: $88,000.00 (0.28% of sales)
* FY2022 Bad Debt Expense: $102,000.00 (0.30% of sales)
* FY2023 Bad Debt Expense: $128,000.00 (including the Great Plains $110k write-off) (0.36% of sales)
* 3-Year Historical Average: $106,000.00 per year.

Customer defaults recur on a cyclical basis across different accounts. Stripping out the bad debt expense of a bankrupt customer while maintaining the gross margin earned on that customer in historical revenue is intellectually inconsistent and artificially inflates maintainable EBITDA.

#### 5. Double-Counting & Balance Sheet Interaction Check
Diligence verified that the Allowance for Doubtful Accounts on the December 31, 2023 balance sheet ($45,000) will be evaluated in the Net Working Capital peg.

#### 6. Mathematical Determination
$$\begin{array}{llr}
\text{Management Claimed Add-Back:} & & \$110,000.00 \\
\text{QoE Tested Amount:} & & \$110,000.00 \\
\text{Historical Operating Risk Adjustment:} & & -\$110,000.00 \\
\hline
\mathbf{Accepted\ QoE\ Add-Back:} & & \mathbf{\$0.00} \\
\mathbf{Rejected\ Amount:} & & \mathbf{\$110,000.00} \\
\text{Classification:} & & \text{Rejected-Addback}

### ADJ-008: Prospective Future 3PL Logistics Cost Savings
* **Category:** Prospective Cost Savings & Synergy Add-Backs (`ADJ-CAT-09`)
* **Financial Statement Line:** COGS / Freight Outbound (`GL 5-2000`)
* **Counterparty:** Echo Global Logistics
* **Document Reference:** `CONTRACT-3PL-2024` / `MGM-SYNERGY-DECK`
* **Period:** FY2023 (Claimed as Full-Year Add-Back)

#### 1. Background & Management Claim
Management proposed a $180,000 add-back in FY2023 for "projected freight cost savings" under a newly negotiated third-party logistics (3PL) master service agreement signed in December 2023, which goes into effect on February 1, 2024. Management claimed that because the rates are contractually locked, historical FY2023 EBITDA should be restated as if these savings were already achieved.

#### 2. Accounting Trace & General Ledger Verification
Diligence verified that **zero dollars** of this claimed $180,000 appeared in the FY2023 general ledger. Freight outbound costs in `GL 5-2000` totaled $2,420,000.00 based on actual carrier rate billings from existing carriers.

#### 3. Source Document Inventory & Audit Trail
* Unimplemented Master Logistics Service Agreement with Echo Global Logistics dated December 18, 2023 (`CONTRACT-3PL-2024`).
* Management Excel spreadsheet estimating a 7.4% reduction in LTL shipping rates across 12 product categories.

#### 4. Analytical Findings & Judgment Discussion
**Diligence rejected this add-back completely from Historical Normalized EBITDA.**
Prospective cost savings, synergies, and future operational efficiencies **do not represent historical earnings**. Adding prospective savings to historical earnings violates standard QoE principles and AICPA guidelines. Achieving these savings involves operational execution risk, volume minimum compliance, fuel surcharge volatility, and carrier performance risks.
While buyers may model these savings in their investment underwriting forecasts, they cannot be baked into historical Normalized EBITDA to calculate enterprise value.

#### 5. Double-Counting & Balance Sheet Interaction Check
Diligence isolated this item to a separate "Pro-Forma Synergy Sensitivity Exhibit" outside the core historical QoE bridge.

#### 6. Mathematical Determination
$$\begin{array}{llr}
\text{Management Claimed Add-Back:} & & \$180,000.00 \\
\text{Historical Actual General Ledger Expense:} & & \$0.00 \\
\hline

### ADJ-009: Revenue Cut-Off Correction (Bill-and-Hold #9842)
* **Category:** Revenue Cut-Off Adjustments (`ADJ-CAT-11`)
* **Financial Statement Line:** Revenue (`GL 4-1000`) & Cost of Goods Sold (`GL 5-1000`)
* **Customer:** Vanguard Aerospace Components
* **Document Reference:** `INV-9842` / `BOL-9842` / `WP-REC-04`
* **Period:** FY2023 (Booked December 31, 2023)

#### 1. Background & Management Claim
Management recognized $240,000.00 of revenue on December 31, 2023, under invoice #9842 issued to Vanguard Aerospace Components. Management included the resulting $152,000.00 gross margin in reported FY2023 EBITDA.

#### 2. Accounting Trace & General Ledger Verification
* Debit: Accounts Receivable (`GL 1-1100`): $240,000.00
* Credit: Gross Commercial Revenue (`GL 4-1000`): $240,000.00
* Debit: Cost of Goods Sold (`GL 5-1000`): $88,000.00
* Credit: Finished Goods Inventory (`GL 1-1300`): $88,000.00
* **Pre-tax EBITDA Impact on Books:** **+$152,000.00**

#### 3. Source Document Inventory & Audit Trail
* Sales Invoice #9842 dated December 31, 2023.
* Bill of Lading #BOL-9842 showing pickup date of **January 6, 2024**, and delivery date of **January 9, 2024**.
* Customer Master Contract specifying shipping terms: **FOB Destination**.
* Physical warehouse year-end count workpaper confirming goods were still sitting on target's staging dock on December 31, 2023.

#### 4. Analytical Findings & Judgment Discussion
Under **ASC 606 (Revenue from Contracts with Customers)**, control does not transfer until delivery occurs under FOB Destination terms. Furthermore, the transaction fails all four SEC and ASC 606 bill-and-hold criteria (no substantive business purpose for holding, customer did not request the hold, goods were not segregated).
The transaction was improperly accelerated into FY2023 to meet year-end bonus and EBITDA thresholds.
The entire gross profit must be reversed from FY2023:
$$\Delta \text{Revenue} = -\$240,000.00, \quad \Delta \text{COGS} = +\$88,000.00 \implies \Delta \text{EBITDA} = -\$152,000.00$$

#### 5. Double-Counting & Balance Sheet Interaction Check
Diligence posted companion audit journal entries to reverse AR by -$240,000 and restore inventory by +$88,000 on the December 31, 2023 balance sheet, preventing distortion of the closing Working Capital peg.

#### 6. Mathematical Determination
$$\begin{array}{llr}
\text{Management Claimed Add-Back:} & & \$0.00\ (\text{Embedded in Baseline}) \\
\text{Premature Revenue Recognized:} & & -\$240,000.00 \\
\text{Reversal of Matching COGS:} & & +\$88,000.00 \\
\hline
\mathbf{Mandatory\ QoE\ EBITDA\ Deduction:} & & \mathbf{-\$152,000.00} \\
\text{Classification:} & & \text{QoE-Mandatory-Deduction}
\end{array}$$

### ADJ-010: Topside Unsupported Management Plug Elimination
* **Category:** Unsupported Manual Topside Entries (`ADJ-CAT-12`)
* **Financial Statement Line:** Other Income (`GL 7-1000`) & Accounts Receivable (`GL 1-1100`)
* **Counterparty:** Internal Management Topside Entry
* **Document Reference:** `JE-2023-12-88` / `EXC-002` (Workstream 03 Tie-Out)
* **Period:** FY2023 (Posted December 31, 2023)

#### 1. Background & Management Claim
In the final December 31, 2023 financial close, the Chief Financial Officer posted manual journal entry #JE-2023-12-88 debiting Accounts Receivable and crediting Other Income for $42,500.00. Management described this entry as "accrued rebate income and vendor volume discounts."

#### 2. Accounting Trace & General Ledger Verification
Direct ledger examination confirmed manual entry:
* `2023-12-31` | JE #2023-12-88 | Debit: `GL 1-1100` $42,500.00 | Credit: `GL 7-1000` $42,500.00

#### 3. Source Document Inventory & Audit Trail

* Exception Log reference `EXC-002` flagged during Workstream 03 (Reconciliation).
* Written inquiry to CFO requesting third-party vendor rebate agreements and calculation workpapers (Inquiry #QOE-INQ-041).

#### 4. Analytical Findings & Judgment Discussion
Management was unable to provide a single vendor contract, confirmation, or invoice supporting the $42,500 credit. Interviews with purchasing personnel confirmed that major suppliers do not offer back-end volume rebates. The entry was an unsupported topside plug posted to hit a budgeted EBITDA target.
Unsupported manual entries posted without audit evidence are completely invalid for transactional purposes and must be reversed in full.

#### 5. Double-Counting & Balance Sheet Interaction Check
Diligence reversed the companion $42,500 debit in Accounts Receivable on the balance sheet, eliminating the uncollectible ghost asset.

#### 6. Mathematical Determination
$$\begin{array}{llr}
\text{Management Claimed Add-Back:} & & \$0.00\ (\text{Embedded in Baseline}) \\
\text{Unsupported Topside Credit Entry:} & & -\$42,500.00 \\
\hline
\mathbf{Mandatory\ QoE\ EBITDA\ Deduction:} & & \mathbf{-\$42,500.00} \\
\text{Classification:} & & \text{QoE-Mandatory-Deduction}
\end{array}$$

### ADJ-011: Duplicate Software Implementation Capitalization
* **Category:** Capitalized Operating Costs & Software Reclassification (`ADJ-CAT-10`)
* **Financial Statement Line:** Balance Sheet — Intangible Software Assets (`GL 1-2000`)
* **Vendor:** NetSuite Implementation Partners
* **Document Reference:** `INV-NS-4410` / `CAPEX-LEDGER-2023`
* **Period:** FY2023

#### 1. Background & Management Claim
Management claimed a $65,000 add-back for third-party consulting fees paid for an ERP system upgrade, asserting that software cutover costs are one-time, non-recurring implementation expenses that should not penalize EBITDA.

#### 2. Accounting Trace & General Ledger Verification
Diligence traced vendor invoice #INV-NS-4410 dated April 14, 2023, for $65,000.00.
**Critical Audit Finding:** The invoice was **never expensed in the P&L**.
The debit was posted directly to:
* Debit: Construction in Progress / Intangible Software (`GL 1-2000`): $65,000.00
* Credit: Accounts Payable (`GL 2-1000`): $65,000.00

#### 3. Source Document Inventory & Audit Trail
* Invoice #INV-NS-4410 from implementation partner.
* Fixed Asset / Software Capitalization Subledger (`CAPEX-LEDGER-2023`).
* Audit workpapers confirming compliance with **ASC 350-40 (Internal-Use Software)**.

#### 4. Analytical Findings & Judgment Discussion
**Diligence rejected this add-back in its entirety.**
Because the $65,000 fee was capitalized to the balance sheet under ASC 350-40, it **never flowed through operating expenses** and did not depress Reported Operating EBITDA. Adding it back to EBITDA would constitute a classic double-counting error, creating $65,000 of fictitious earnings out of thin air.

#### 5. Double-Counting & Balance Sheet Interaction Check
No balance sheet adjustment is required; the asset remains on the balance sheet subject to future amortization (which is excluded from EBITDA by definition).

#### 6. Mathematical Determination
$$\begin{array}{llr}
\text{Management Claimed Add-Back:} & & \$65,000.00 \\
\text{Amount Expensed in Operating P&L:} & & \$0.00 \\
\text{Amount Capitalized on Balance Sheet:} & & \$65,000.00 \\
\hline
\mathbf{Accepted\ QoE\ Add-Back:} & & \mathbf{\$0.00} \\
\mathbf{Rejected\ Amount:} & & \mathbf{\$65,000.00} \\
\text{Classification:} & & \text{Rejected-Addback}
\end{array}$$

### ADJ-012: Unsubstantiated Growth Consulting Retainers
* **Category:** Discretionary Consulting & Professional Fees (`ADJ-CAT-07`)

#### 1. Background & Management Claim
Management claimed a $75,000 add-back for "discretionary growth strategy consulting," asserting that these fees represented a non-recurring corporate re-branding and strategic sales transformation study that will terminate prior to acquisition.

#### 2. Accounting Trace & General Ledger Verification

#### 3. Source Document Inventory & Audit Trail
* Management could only produce a single invoice (`INV-BR-101`) for $25,000.00, dated March 12, 2023.

Clear. No interaction with balance sheet or working capital.
