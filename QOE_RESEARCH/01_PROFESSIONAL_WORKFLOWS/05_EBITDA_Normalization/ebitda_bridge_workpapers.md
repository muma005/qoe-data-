## 1. Executive Summary & Core Transaction Bridge

During financial due diligence on Apex Global Industrial Holding Inc. ("Apex" or the "Target"), the buy-side QoE team audited management's proposed Adjusted EBITDA calculations presented in the Confidential Information Memorandum (CIM).

Management presented a reported FY2023 Operating EBITDA of **$3,330,000.00** and proposed **$788,000.00 in adjustments**, claiming an Adjusted EBITDA of **$4,118,000.00**.

Rigorous audit procedures, contract analysis, and forensic transaction testing resulted in:
* **Accepted QoE Add-Backs:** **+$530,000.00** (gross verified)
* **Less: Mandatory Replacement Costs:** **-$250,000.00** (FMV replacement CEO)
* **Accepted Net Add-Backs:** **+$280,000.00**
* **Mandatory QoE EBITDA Deductions:** **-$249,500.00** (insurance gain, bill-and-hold cut-off, topside plug)
* **Net Diligence Adjustments:** **+$280,500.00**
* **QoE Normalized EBITDA:** **$3,610,500.00**
* **Management EBITDA Overstatement ("Haircut"):** **-$507,500.00** (12.3% reduction against seller claim)
