## 1. Evidentiary Hierarchy & Standard of Balance Sheet Proof

In working capital and net debt due diligence, representations by company owners or investment bankers regarding asset values or unrecorded liabilities are **inadmissible without third-party documentary corroboration**.

```
+────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
|                                    EVIDENTIARY HIERARCHY                                           |
|                                                                                                    |
|   [TIER 1] EXECUTED THIRD-PARTY LEGAL & BANK SETTLEMENT PROOF (Highest Reliability)                |
|   • Formal bank payoff letters specifying per diem interest and UCC lien discharge commitments.    |
|   • Third-party bank confirmation letters, signed escrow agreements, IRS/state tax audit notices.  |
|                                                                                                    |
|   [TIER 2] VERIFIED TRANSACTIONAL SUBLEDGERS & CONTRACTS (High Reliability)                        |
|   • Granular Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable subledger agings reconciled to the GL.       |
|   • Executed vendor supply agreements, customer MSAs, signed physical inventory count tags.        |
|                                                                                                    |
|   [TIER 3] INDEPENDENT TECHNICAL & APPRAISAL REPORTS (Moderate-High Reliability)                   |
|   • Certified machinery engineering condition reports, environmental Phase I liability surveys.    |
|   • Actuarial defined-benefit pension valuation memos, third-party sales tax nexus surveys.        |
|                                                                                                    |
|   [TIER 4] INTERNAL MANAGEMENT ACCRUAL SCHEDULES & MODELS (Conditional / Needs Verification)       |
|   • Monthly balance sheet workpapers, internal inventory reserve calculation spreadsheets.         |
|   • Internal payroll registers, unvetted warranty reserve estimates, draft lease schedules.        |
|                                                                                                    |
|   [TIER 5] UNSUBSTANTIATED ORAL REPRESENTATIONS (Inadmissible for Quality of Earnings)             |
|   • Management claims: "Vendors don't mind Net 90", "All our inventory is fast-moving".           |
+────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
```

## 2. Professional Judgment & Verification Matrix

The following matrix governs the evidentiary testing, red flags, and review requirements for working capital and debt diligence:

| Diligence Focus Area | Primary Documentary Evidence | Secondary / Corroborating Evidence | Forensic Red Flags & Disallowance Triggers | Mandatory Audit Verification Procedure | Requisite Sign-Off Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Balance Sheet Tie-Out** | Monthly General Ledger trial balances, Audited balance sheets | Bank reconciliations, Journal voucher audit logs | Unreconciled balancing plugs; out-of-period adjustments; balance sheet imbalance ($\text{Assets} \ne \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity}$). | Extract 24 consecutive month-end trial balances; verify debits equal credits; map accounts into NWC, Debt, Cash, and Excluded buckets. | Senior Analyst + Manager |
| **Stretched Accounts Payable** | Accounts Payable subledger aging reports, Vendor invoices | Master supplier contracts, Vendor credit hold notices | Sharp increase in 60+ and 90+ day AP prior to sale; vendors threatening stop-shipment; DPO expanding >5 days in trailing quarter. | Stratify AP aging by vendor; compare against agreed payment terms; reclassify all payables past terms from NWC to Debt-Like Items. | Manager + Partner |
| **Accounts Receivable Aging & Reserves** | Accounts Receivable subledger aging, Bankruptcy court notices | Customer correspondence, Cash collection remittance slips | Allowance for doubtful accounts decreases while overdue balances rise; bankrupt customer balances kept on balance sheet. | Roll forward historical bad debt losses; compute required reserve by aging bracket; normalize closing AR for reserve deficiency. | Senior Analyst + Manager |
| **Inventory Valuation & Obsolescence** | Perpetual inventory subledgers by SKU, Trailing 12M sales logs | Physical inventory count sheets, Scrap logs, Production bills | High inventory carrying value with zero unit sales in trailing 12+ months; scrap logged on shop floor but omitted from general ledger. | Calculate Months of Supply (MOS) by SKU; apply 50% reserve to slow-moving and 100% to dead stock; adjust inventory carrying value in NWC. | Manager + Partner |
| **Target Working Capital Peg** | 24-month normalized monthly NWC model, Monthly revenue ledgers | Run-rate financial forecast, Customer contract billing schedules | Seller proposing historical trough peg to trigger post-closing cash true-up; ignoring recent operational scale expansion. | Model LTM average, 6-month average, seasonal peg, and percentage-of-revenue peg; defend run-rate aligned peg with collar. | Quality Partner |
| **Funded Bank Borrowings** | Executed credit agreements, Formal bank payoff letters | Bank debt statements, Wire transfer records, UCC-1 lien filings | Omission of revolving credit balances; unaccrued per diem interest; missing payoff letters for collateral liens. | Inspect credit agreements; obtain executed payoff letters directly from lenders; confirm per diem interest through closing. | Manager + Partner |
| **Accrued Transaction Bonuses** | Executed transaction bonus agreements, Board resolutions | Advisory engagement letters, Payroll tax withholding registers | Change-of-control bonuses buried in general payroll accruals; employer payroll tax liability omitted. | Inspect board minutes; obtain all transaction bonus agreements; add employer FICA/Medicare tax load; deduct as Debt-Like Item. | Manager + Partner |
| **State Sales & Use Tax Exposure** | Historical sales by state, State tax audit assessments | Customer resale exemption certificates, Wayfair nexus surveys | Sales into states exceeding economic nexus thresholds ($100k / 200 txns) without registration or exemption certificates. | Perform 50-state nexus assessment; quantify untaxed sales; apply state tax rates plus statutory penalties; deduct as Debt-Like Item. | Manager + Partner |
| **Unfunded PTO / Vacation** | Detailed employee census, HR employee handbook | Historical PTO balance logs, Bi-weekly payroll summaries | Vacation balances tracked informally on departmental spreadsheets without general ledger balance sheet accrual. | Multiply accrued unused vacation hours by loaded hourly compensation rates; calculate unaccrued liability; deduct as Debt-Like Item. | Senior Analyst + Manager |
| **Customer Deferred Revenue Haircut** | Deferred revenue subledgers, Master customer contracts | Hosting cost schedules, Account management time logs | Seller collects multi-year cash upfront, leaving buyer with post-closing fulfillment obligations without ongoing cash flow. | Quantify direct post-closing fulfillment costs plus normal operating margin under ASC 805; deduct as Debt-Like Item. | Manager + Partner |
| **Machinery Deferred Maintenance** | Independent machinery engineering inspection reports | Equipment maintenance logs, Vendor repair quotes | Preventative maintenance halted 12 months prior to sale; critical machinery operating on deferred service status. | Inspect equipment maintenance logs; obtain certified engineering quotes for overdue overhauls; negotiate Debt-Like deduction. | Manager + Partner |
| **Restricted / Trapped Cash** | Standby letter of credit agreements, Bank escrow agreements | Bank collateral statements, Foreign exchange restrictions | Presenting 100% of cash on hand as unrestricted while cash is pledged as collateral for workers comp or leases. | Inspect bank accounts for restricted covenants; identify collateralized cash; deduct restricted cash from cash additions. | Senior Analyst + Manager |

## 3. Workpaper Cross-Referencing & Audit Trail Standards

To maintain numerical traceability throughout the simulator, every balance sheet and debt workpaper must maintain strict alphanumeric cross-referencing:

```
[SRC-BS-xxx] Raw Monthly Balance Sheet / Trial Balance
   │
   ▼
[PREP-BS] Standardized & Cleansed Balance Sheet (Workstream 02)
   │
   ▼
[REC-BS] Balance Sheet Reconciliation Workpaper (Workstream 03)
   │
   ▼
[ANA-BS] Historical Balance Sheet Movement Model (Workstream 04)
   │
   ▼
[WC-xxx] Working Capital Normalization Schedule (Workstream 07)
   ├── [DEBT-xxx] Debt & Debt-Like Item Verification Workpaper
   ├── [PEG-xxx] Working Capital Target Peg Comparison Model
   └── [EVB-xxx] Master Enterprise Value to Equity Value Bridge Line Item
```

### Standard Audit Tickmark Index

* $\checkmark$ : **Traced to Balance Sheet Control Account:** Confirmed amount agrees exactly with general ledger trial balance.
* $\Phi$ : **Vouched to Third-Party Statement / Payoff Letter:** Inspected bank payoff letter, lender statement, or tax notice.
* $\Lambda$ : **Mathematical Footing Checked:** Verified column, row, and formula calculations foot without rounding error.
* $\Omega$ : **Benchmarked to Contractual Terms:** Reconciled to vendor credit terms, master agreement, or loan covenant.
* $\Psi$ : **Tripartite Settlement Firewall Checked:** Confirmed zero duplication between EBITDA, NWC, and Net Debt.
* $\Theta$ : **Mandatory Purchase Price Haircut:** Technical correction or commercial liability deducting dollar-for-dollar from EV.
