## 3. SPA Exhibit B — Accounting Principles Schedule

The following text represents the definitive contractual language for **Exhibit B (Accounting Principles Schedule)** of the Stock Purchase Agreement, drafted by transaction counsel in direct alignment with the Quality of Earnings report:

```markdown
### EXHIBIT B: ACCOUNTING PRINCIPLES AND WORKING CAPITAL CALCULATION METHODOLOGY

1. Governing Accounting Principles
This Statement of Accounting Principles governs the determination of Estimated Closing Net Working Capital, 
Final Closing Net Working Capital, Indebtedness, and Cash pursuant to Section 2.5 and Section 2.6 of this Agreement.
Final Closing Net Working Capital shall be prepared and calculated in accordance with:
(a) The specific accounting methodologies, policies, categorizations, and definitions set forth in Part 2 of this Exhibit B;
(b) To the extent not inconsistent with clause (a), the accounting principles, practices, and estimations applied by the 
    Company in the preparation of the Audited Balance Sheet as of December 31, 2022; and
(c) To the extent not inconsistent with clauses (a) and (b), United States Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (US GAAP).
In the event of any conflict between the provisions of clause (a), clause (b), and clause (c), the provisions of clause (a) 
shall strictly govern and control.

2. Specific Diligence Accounting Principles and Exclusions
(a) Current Assets shall comprise solely:
    (i) Trade Accounts Receivable (General Ledger Accounts 1100-1199), net of an Allowance for Doubtful Accounts calculated 
        in accordance with historical aging schedules, but strictly excluding General Ledger Account 1150 (Unbilled Bill-and-Hold 
        Receivables, specifically Invoice #9842 in the amount of $240,000.00);
    (ii) Inventories (General Ledger Accounts 1200-1299), net of an Excess and Obsolete Reserve of not less than $270,000.00, 
         and strictly excluding any unevidenced manual topside journal entries (specifically Journal Entry #JE-12-88 in the 
         amount of $55,000.00); and
    (iii) Prepaid Expenses and Operating Deposits (General Ledger Accounts 1300-1399) incurred in the ordinary course of business.
(b) Excluded Current Assets: Current Assets shall strictly exclude (i) Cash and Cash Equivalents, (ii) Income Tax Receivables 
    (specifically including the Employee Retention Credit receivable of $42,000.00), and (iii) Intercompany Receivables.
(c) Current Liabilities shall comprise solely:
    (i) Trade Accounts Payable (General Ledger Accounts 2000-2099) incurred in the ordinary course of business, strictly 
        excluding past-due trade payables greater than sixty (60) days past due ($250,000.00), which shall be treated as Indebtedness;
    (ii) Accrued Operating Expenses (General Ledger Accounts 2100-2199); and
    (iii) Customer Deposits and ordinary course operational payroll withholdings.
(d) Excluded Current Liabilities: Current Liabilities shall strictly exclude (i) any item defined as Indebtedness or Seller 
    Transaction Expenses, (ii) accrued change-of-control transaction bonuses and M&A legal advisory fees ($145,000.00), 
    (iii) unremitted state sales and use tax audit liabilities ($68,000.00), (iv) accrued unfunded vacation/PTO ($45,000.00), 
    (v) customer deferred revenue fulfillment costs under ASC 805 ($110,000.00), and (vi) CNC machinery deferred maintenance ($60,000.00).

3. Target Net Working Capital Peg and Collar Band
The Target Net Working Capital Peg is agreed to be exactly $4,300,000.00 (the "Target Peg").
The parties agree to a symmetrical Collar Band of $75,000.00 above and below the Target Peg.
(a) If Final Closing Net Working Capital is between $4,225,000.00 and $4,375,000.00 (inclusive), the Working Capital Adjustment 
    shall be exactly $0.00, and neither Buyer nor Seller shall owe any true-up payment.
(b) If Final Closing Net Working Capital exceeds $4,375,000.00, Buyer shall pay Seller an amount equal to (Final Closing NWC - $4,375,000.00).
(c) If Final Closing Net Working Capital is less than $4,225,000.00, Seller shall pay Buyer an amount equal to ($4,225,000.00 - Final Closing NWC), 
    disbursed first from the Working Capital Escrow Account.
```
