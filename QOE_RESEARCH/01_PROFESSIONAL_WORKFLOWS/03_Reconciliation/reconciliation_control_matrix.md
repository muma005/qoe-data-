# Quality of Earnings (QoE) Research Programme
## Workstream 03 — Financial Reconciliation
### Deliverable 4: Reconciliation Control and Evidence Matrix

This matrix establishes the non-negotiable operational controls, evidentiary standards, failure triggers, and sign-off hierarchy governing all financial reconciliations in a professional Quality of Earnings (QoE) / Financial Due Diligence (FDD) engagement.

---

### Master Reconciliation Control Matrix

| Procedure ID & Name | Financial & Transaction Risk Addressed | Control / Validation Performed | Mandatory Primary Evidence Required | Failure Condition / Escalation Trigger | Next Investigative Action | Reviewer Responsibility | Completion Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RPC-01: Trial Balance to General Ledger Tie-Out** | Incomplete ledger extraction, orphan GL accounts, unposted draft journals, post-close tampering. | Automated SQL join between GL detail debits/credits and TB movements by account code and fiscal period. | EVD-001: Direct ERP database export logs with system timestamps, GL batch audit trail. | Variance != .00 on any account line; unbalanced debits and credits. | Filter GL for unposted/draft batches; audit journal entry sequence numbers for missing lines; re-run database extraction query. | **Preparer:** Staff Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Sign-off:** Engagement Manager | 100% mathematical match (Variance == 0.00) across all accounts in all periods. |
| **RPC-02: TB to Management Accounts Reconciliation** | Topside earnings manipulation, unposted Excel overrides, distorted run-rate EBITDA presented to buyers. | Line-by-line mapping of trial balance accounts to management reporting pack categories; variance recalculation. | EVD-002: Client CFO month-end close workbooks, Board presentation source files, GL manual journal vouchers. | Unexplained variance between Board deck EBITDA and TB EBITDA >,000. | Demand CFO bridge schedule; inspect management Excel formulas for hardcoded plugs; trace off-ledger adjustments to supporting invoices. | **Preparer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Engagement Manager<br>**Sign-off:** Lead TAS Partner | Every variance line is supported by documented management rationale and classified for QoE EBITDA adjustment. |
| **RPC-03: Mgmt Accounts to Audited FS Bridge** | Aggressive interim accounting policies, unrecorded year-end audit adjustments, lack of financial integrity. | Annual bridge linking 12-month internal management accounts to audited financial statements; audit adjustment mapping. | EVD-003: Signed Auditor Report, Auditor AJE Schedule (Schedule of Unadjusted/Adjusted Differences), Management Letter. | Unreconciled residual variance >; management failure to provide auditor AJE log. | Interview external audit partner; review audit working papers on inventory valuation, revenue cut-off, and bonus accruals. | **Preparer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Engagement Manager<br>**Sign-off:** Lead TAS Partner | 100% of variance bridged by verified auditor AJEs; permanent disconnects flagged for QoE normalization. |
| **RPC-04: Consolidation & Intercompany Elimination** | Fictitious intercompany revenue, hidden trading losses in subsidiaries, unrecorded loans, tax transfer pricing exposure. | N x N matrix reconciling all bilateral intercompany receivables, payables, sales, management fees, and loan balances. | EVD-004: Entity-pair account statements, cross-border shipping bills of lading, executed intercompany promissory notes. | Net intercompany break !=  upon group consolidation; unbalanced intercompany debt balances. | Trace in-transit inventory and cash wires; revalue foreign currency balances at closing spot rates; recalculate intercompany markup eliminations. | **Preparer:** Staff Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Sign-off:** Engagement Manager | Consolidated net intercompany variance == .00, or residual items isolated into documented Net Debt adjustments. |
| **RPC-05: Revenue Subledger to GL Tie-Out** | Cut-off acceleration, bill-and-hold fraud, fictitious manual revenue credits, unrecorded sales returns. | Reconciliation of detailed invoice-level sales registers to credits in GL revenue accounts; cut-off testing. | EVD-005: ERP transactional sales registers, customer purchase orders, warehouse bills of lading, proof of delivery. | Subledger sales != GL revenue; manual journal entries debiting/crediting revenue accounts. | Sample 100% of revenue entries posted within 5 days before/after period end; verify delivery execution; test subsequent cash collections. | **Preparer:** Staff Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Sign-off:** Engagement Manager | Subledger sales equal GL revenue; all manual revenue entries investigated and verified or marked for QoE EBITDA reversal. |
| **RPC-06: AR Aging Subledger to GL Control Tie-Out** | Fictitious receivables, unrecorded bad debts, hidden customer disputes, manual control account plugs. | Comparison of customer-level open AR aging summary to GL account 1-1100; age-bracket analysis; dispute review. | EVD-006: Detailed AR aging report as-of balance sheet date, bank deposit slips, customer legal dispute notices. | AR subledger total != GL control balance; manual entries posted directly to GL control account. | Audit GL control account for non-subledger journal vouchers; inspect unapplied customer cash suspense; recalculate bad debt allowance. | **Preparer:** Staff Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Sign-off:** Engagement Manager | Subledger ties to GL; customer disputes and uncollectible debts quantified for Working Capital / Net Debt haircuts. |
| **RPC-07: AP Aging Subledger to GL Control Tie-Out** | Unrecorded liabilities, suppressed operating expenses, aged clearing balances, cut-off manipulation. | Comparison of vendor aging report and GRNI clearing register to GL trade AP accounts (2-1000 & 2-1050). | EVD-007: Detailed AP aging as-of balance sheet date, GRNI open PO listing, subsequent bank disbursement journals. | AP subledger + GRNI != GL control account; open GRNI items >60 days old; unrecorded supplier bills. | Search for unrecorded liabilities by auditing disbursements 45 days post-period; inspect warehouse receiving logs for unvouchered deliveries. | **Preparer:** Staff Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Sign-off:** Engagement Manager | All open vendor balances reconciled; unrecorded liabilities accrued; aged GRNI cleared or classified. |
| **RPC-08: Inventory Subledger to GL Roll-Forward** | Phantom stock, unrecorded scrap, capitalized operating overhead, obsolete inventory overstated on balance sheet. | Roll-forward of perpetual stock register values (RM, WIP, FG) to GL inventory accounts; physical count reconciliation. | EVD-008: Perpetual inventory stock valuation reports, physical count sheets, standard cost update audit trails. | Inventory roll-forward variance != ; physical count book-to-physical variance >1.5%; negative stock quantities. | Reconcile physical stocktake count variances; audit lower-of-cost-or-market valuation test; verify standard cost overhead absorption formulas. | **Preparer:** Staff Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Sign-off:** Engagement Manager | Perpetual inventory ties to GL; book-to-physical variances explained; obsolescence reserve validated against aging. |
| **RPC-09: Bank Statement to GL Four-Column Proof of Cash** | Cash kiting, unrecorded bank accounts, unauthorized withdrawals, float distortion, fictitious cash balances. | Substantive 4-column reconciliation across beginning cash, receipts, disbursements, and ending cash between bank and GL. | EVD-009: Direct bank statements for all operational accounts, third-party bank confirmation letters, subsequent clearing proofs. | Reconciled bank balance != reconciled book balance across any of the 4 columns; stale checks >90 days old. | Trace deposits in transit to bank clearance within 3 days; inspect outstanding check clearing history; examine bank debit/credit memos. | **Preparer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Engagement Manager<br>**Sign-off:** Lead TAS Partner | Zero variance across all four columns (Variance == 0.00); all reconciling items verified via subsequent clearing statements. |
| **RPC-10: Payroll Census & Tax Filings to GL Payroll Bridge** | Off-payroll wages, under-accrued bonuses, executive personal expenses, payroll tax non-compliance. | Reconciliation of quarterly payroll tax returns (IRS Form 941/W-2) and third-party processor summaries to GL compensation accounts. | EVD-010: Quarterly IRS Form 941 filings, third-party payroll provider annual registers (ADP/Paychex), executive compensation contracts. | Form 941 total wages != GL direct and indirect payroll expense; unaccrued accrued vacation liabilities. | Cross-reference employee census to payroll register; verify bonus pool calculation against board approvals; test payroll tax clearing accounts. | **Preparer:** Staff Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Sign-off:** Engagement Manager | 100% of payroll variance explained; accrued bonuses and payroll tax exposure quantified for Net Debt. |
| **RPC-11: Fixed Asset Register (FAR) to GL Roll-Forward** | Ghost assets, expensed CapEx to depress EBITDA, capitalized maintenance to inflate EBITDA, tax vs GAAP mismatch. | Roll-forward of gross fixed asset costs, additions, disposals, and accumulated depreciation from FAR to GL asset accounts. | EVD-011: Detailed FAR asset register, vendor equipment purchase invoices, physical scrap/sale disposal receipts. | FAR cost/acc dep != GL accounts; lack of physical asset verification for top 20 assets; depreciation expense discrepancies. | Sample CapEx invoices > to verify proper capitalization; conduct physical existence audit on plant floor; recalculate straight-line depreciation. | **Preparer:** Staff Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Sign-off:** Engagement Manager | FAR ties to GL; ghost assets written off; CapEx categorized into Growth vs Maintenance for cash flow analysis. |
| **RPC-12: Debt Schedule & Lender Confirmations to GL** | Unrecorded debt, omitted shareholder loans, capital lease understatements, debt covenant violations. | Roll-forward of debt principal, interest accruals, and repayments; comparison to third-party lender statements and credit agreements. | EVD-012: Executed credit agreements, monthly lender payoff/balance statements, debt confirmation certificates. | Ending debt principal != lender statements; unaccrued interest expense; unrecorded loan covenants. | Review bank confirmation responses; inspect cash receipts for unrecorded loan proceeds; verify compliance with financial covenant ratios. | **Preparer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Engagement Manager<br>**Sign-off:** Lead TAS Partner | 100% of debt facilities confirmed; debt-like items quantified for transaction purchase price deduction. |
| **RPC-13: Tax Return (Schedule M-1/M-3) to Financial Statements** | Dual books, undisclosed aggressive tax shelters, unrecorded state/federal tax liabilities, earnings quality red flags. | Comparison of Line 1 Book Net Income on Form 1120 Schedule M-1/M-3 to financial statement net income; permanent/temporary mapping. | EVD-013: Filed IRS Form 1120/1065 corporate returns with IRS e-file acceptance acknowledgments, Schedule M-3 workpapers. | Book net income per tax return != financial statement net income; unexplained book-to-tax adjustments. | Demand tax preparer workpapers; verify depreciation timing differences (MACRS vs GAAP); review state income tax nexus and exposure. | **Preparer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Engagement Manager<br>**Sign-off:** Lead TAS Partner | Book income reconciled; permanent and temporary differences verified; potential tax indemnities identified. |
| **RPC-14: SaaS Deferred Revenue Waterfall to Balance Sheet** | Premature subscription revenue recognition, immediate setup fee recognition, ARR/MRR inflation, ASC 606 violation. | Roll-forward of customer deferred revenue: Beg Deferred + Billings - Recognized Revenue = Ending Deferred; match to GL A/C 2-3000. | EVD-014: SaaS contract billing waterfall, Stripe/Chargebee transaction logs, executed customer Master Service Agreements (MSAs). | Waterfall ending deferred revenue != GL balance; setup fees recognized upfront without standalone value. | Recalculate daily ratable amortization schedules across customer cohorts; inspect contract cancellation/refund clauses; audit webhook syncs. | **Preparer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Engagement Manager<br>**Sign-off:** Lead TAS Partner | Deferred revenue schedule matches GL; revenue recognition adjusted to strict ASC 606 ratable compliance. |
| **RPC-15: GRNI Aged Clearing Reconciliation** | Stale purchasing accruals used as an earnings cookie jar, unrecorded vendor credits, inventory valuation error. | Aging of open purchase order receipts in the GRNI clearing account; matching against vendor invoices and receiving slips. | EVD-015: Open GRNI PO subledger reports, dock receiving slips, vendor confirmation statements. | GRNI items older than 60 days without vendor billing; duplicate receipts remaining open. | Match aged GRNI lines against vendor statements; cancel duplicate receipts; release stale accruals as non-operating items. | **Preparer:** Staff Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Sign-off:** Engagement Manager | Active GRNI verified as genuine goods in transit or unbilled deliveries; aged balances eliminated from Working Capital. |
| **RPC-16: Manufacturing COGM & Overhead Absorption** | Over-absorption of overhead capitalizing current opex into inventory, standard cost distortions, hidden production scrap. | Reconciliation of raw material usage, direct labor, and applied overhead to work-in-progress and finished goods; variance analysis. | EVD-016: Plant work orders, Bills of Materials (BOMs), direct labor payroll cards, factory overhead spending ledgers. | Significant unabsorbed or over-absorbed overhead variance left in balance sheet inventory; unposted scrap. | Recalculate actual vs standard overhead rates; adjust inventory valuation for over-absorbed fixed overhead; review scrap logs. | **Preparer:** Senior Analyst<br>**Reviewer:** Engagement Manager<br>**Sign-off:** Lead TAS Partner | COGM roll-forward ties to finished goods additions; overhead absorption normalized in period EBITDA. |
